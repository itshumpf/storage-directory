The daily repricing pulse

Every street-rate move the network made, caught by diffing consecutive daily scrapes SKU by SKU. On 2026-07-11: 2,443 units repriced — 247 hikes (median +23.7%) against 2,196 cuts (median -6.5%), plus 3,194 promotion switches. Cuts outnumber hikes but hikes run steeper — the signature of algorithmic yield management: trim soft inventory broadly, squeeze scarce units hard. This series gets a point every scrape day.
