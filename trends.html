Public Storage (NYSE: PSA) closing share price

PSA's official daily closing price, plotted on the same scraper snapshot dates as the charts above so the x-axes line up.

How non-trading days are handled. The scraper publishes every calendar day; the market does not. Each snapshot date shows PSA's most recent close on or before that date. A filled dot is a close from a trading day not already plotted. A hollow dot is the same close repeated onto a later snapshot date because no newer close existed yet — a carried-forward value, not that day's price. Where the most recent close is more than 5 days old the point is left blank and the line breaks rather than continuing flat. The daily job runs at 11:00 UTC, ahead of the 16:00 ET close, so the newest close available on any snapshot date is normally the previous trading day's. Because the series is drawn on snapshot dates, closes from trading days with no snapshot are not plotted.

60 of 60 snapshot dates carry a price: 44 are a close from a trading day not already plotted, 16 repeat the previous close (weekend, market holiday, or no newer close at scrape time), and 0 are left blank because the newest close available was more than 5 days old. Latest plotted point: 2026-09-11 showing the close of 2026-09-11 ($296.48).
