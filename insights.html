The promo decoder — what the discounts are really worth

The tracked operator runs exactly three promotions network-wide. Averaged over the first three months, the famous '$1 first month' and '2nd Month Free' are near-identical (~33% off), while 'First month 50% off' is barely half the discount it sounds like (~17%). Notice the tiering: the cheapest units get the '$1' offer, the priciest get '2nd Month Free' — the promo itself signals the unit's price band. What the advertised price range represents

Every unit page shows a min-max price range that looks like a pricing band. We tested whether street rates move within it. They don't — across 58,233 listings, 47.6% sit exactly at the midpoint, because the displayed range is mechanically today's price ±20% (rounded). It's best read as a disclaimer construct rather than an independent pricing signal: when the range moves, that reflects the price moving. Real rate movement is tracked on the daily trends page.
