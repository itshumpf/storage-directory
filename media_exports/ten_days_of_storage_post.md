At one CubeSmart in Topeka, 40% off became 50% off—and the online rent stayed around $43.

The in-store comparison price rose from $71 to $86. First month free remained attached.

StorageMart took another route: 622 of 652 comparable offers gaining deeper three-month discounts raised recurring rates—typically about 10%.

Modeled three-month total cost fell 45.3%. Twelve-month total cost rose 1.7%.

Meanwhile, SmartStop cut advertised web rates broadly. Different moves that national price lines can compress—or miss entirely.
